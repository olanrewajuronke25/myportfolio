01 — About

A psychologist's read on human behaviour, applied to business data.

I graduated from Adekunle Ajasin University with a Second Class Upper Division in Pure and Applied Psychology — training that taught me to look past the surface of numbers and ask why people behave the way the data shows.

That lens has shaped every role since: as a Productivity Manager at Tangerine Life Insurance, I turned team performance data into reports that guided real decisions. As a Financial Advisor, I used client data to match people with the right products. As an Office Assistant, I kept the records straight that everything else depended on.

Today I'm channelling that experience into data analysis — Excel modelling, CRM insight, and performance reporting — with the same goal that has always driven me: helping teams make better decisions, faster.
